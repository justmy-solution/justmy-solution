## 💫 About Us

**JustMy** is a software development agency focused on building reliable, scalable digital products for startups and growing businesses.

We work across the full stack — from backend architecture and APIs to modern frontend applications — with a strong focus on clean code, performance, and long-term maintainability. Whether you need a greenfield MVP, a platform rebuild, or ongoing product development, we ship production-ready software.

---

## 🚀 What We Do

- **Full-stack web development** — React, Next.js, Vue, and modern frontend ecosystems
- **Backend & APIs** — Node.js, Python, PHP, microservices, and system architecture
- **Payments & billing** — Stripe, subscriptions, and revenue infrastructure
- **AI & automation** — LLM integrations, workflows, and intelligent product features
- **SaaS & product engineering** — From prototype to production and beyond

## 👥 Our Team

### Duy Nguyen — CTO & Founder

Senior full-stack and AI engineer with **8+ years** of experience building SaaS platforms, cloud-native applications, and AI-powered products. Duy leads technical strategy, architecture, and delivery at JustMy — from greenfield products to production-scale platforms.

**Focus areas**
- SaaS architecture, multi-tenant systems, and cloud-native backends
- AI product engineering — RAG, agentic workflows, and conversational AI
- Full-stack delivery across NestJS, React, Next.js, Vue, PostgreSQL, and AWS

**Selected work**
- **AskSKY!** — Production RAG-powered AI platform for building domain-specific assistants from websites, documents, and proprietary knowledge bases
- **AI customer engagement** — Multilingual conversational AI and agentic workflows for onboarding, lead qualification, and support automation
- **Multi-tenant SaaS** — Quoting, scheduling, billing, and payment automation platforms for operational business workflows

Prior experience includes lead development roles at **SquaredFinancial** and full-stack product work at **SafariLawnCare**, with a track record of shipping measurable improvements in delivery speed, automation, and product reliability across remote engineering teams.
